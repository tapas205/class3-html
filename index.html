Welcome to Central Wangaratta Motel

Central Wangaratta Motel in Wangaratta offers rooms with air-conditioning, private bathrooms and free WiFi. All rooms are equipped with a kettle, bar, fridge, toaster, microwave and hair dryer. Crockery and cutlery are included as part of our service for guest use. Centrally located, just 500 metres from the Wangaratta Performing Arts Centre, Central Wangaratta Motel offers comfortable and affordable accommodation with free Wi-Fi and free private parking. The motel features well-appointed family rooms, making it an ideal choice for both leisure and business travellers.
